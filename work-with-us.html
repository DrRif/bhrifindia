Work with us

We are early. That is the useful part — the people who join now shape what the Foundation becomes rather than inheriting it.

Students and early-career researchers

Learn to do research properly, on a real question

We run short-term studentships and mentored projects for MBBS, allied health and postgraduate students — including support for ICMR STS applications and other competitive studentships. You get a defined question, a supervisor who reads your drafts, and a realistic path to authorship. We are not looking for people to run our data entry.

  • Short-term studentships and mentored research projects
  • Support with protocol writing, ethics submissions and analysis
  • Training in research methodology, biostatistics and AI literacy

Write to us with a paragraph on what interests you and where you study. A polished proposal is not required.

Medical colleges, hospitals and universities

Join a multi-institutional study, or host a pipeline

Several of our programmes only work at multi-site scale — the AI education trials, the AMR surveillance network, the vector-borne disease pipeline. We handle protocol development, ethics documentation, data management and analysis; partner sites contribute setting, participants and local investigators, with the authorship and co-investigator position that goes with it.

  • Co-investigator roles on ICMR, NMC, DBT and comparable proposals
  • Site participation in multi-centre studies across South India
  • Adoption and evaluation of our AI medical education platform
  • Laboratory partnerships for AMR surveillance
Funders, CSR and philanthropy

Fund infrastructure, not just individual studies

Competitive grants pay for studies. They rarely pay for the thing that makes the next twenty studies cheaper: a maintained database, a validated instrument, a trained field team. CSR and philanthropic support is most useful to us exactly there — and it is the support that compounds.

  • Programme funding for surveillance and data infrastructure
  • Named studentships and fellowships
  • Equipment and laboratory capacity at partner institutions
  • Community screening and health education in defined districts

Section 12A, 80G and CSR eligibility will be applied for after incorporation; current status is published on the compliance page.

Device developers and manufacturers

Independent validation for health technology

Our testing and certification function is in development and not yet an operating service. If you are building a wearable, digital or point-of-care health technology and want independent performance validation on Indian populations, tell us now — the protocols we write should be shaped by the devices that will actually be tested against them.
